Resumen

Carboxypeptidase B2 (EC 3.4.17.20) (Carboxypeptidase U) (CPU) (Plasma carboxypeptidase B) (pCPB) (Thrombin-activable fibrinolysis inhibitor) (TAFI) Gen en la corrida: CPB2. Gen en UniProt: CPB2. Revisada: reviewed. Rai 2025Data-Independent Acquisition Analysis to Identify Serum Biomarkers and Neoantigens for Differentiating Colorectal Cancer from Inflammatory Bowel Diseases 10.1021/acs.jproteome.5c00285

Función

FUNCTION: Cleaves C-terminal arginine or lysine residues from biologically active peptides such as kinins or anaphylatoxins in the circulation thereby regulating their activities. Down-regulates fibrinolysis by removing C-terminal lysine residues from fibrin that has already been partially degraded by plasmin. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10574983}.
