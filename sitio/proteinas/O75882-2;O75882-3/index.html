Función

FUNCTION: Substrate-recognition component of a membrane-tethered ubiquitin ligase complex that acts as a negative regulator of melanocortin receptor signaling by mediating ubiquitination of MC1R and MC4R (By similarity). Within the complex, MEGF8 both recognizes MC1R and MC4R and recruits the cytoplasmic E3 ubiquitin ligase MGRN1 (By similarity). Involved in the initial immune cell clustering during inflammatory response and may regulate chemotactic activity of chemokines (PubMed:9736737). Has a critical role in normal myelination in the central nervous system (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q99J86, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9WU60, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9736737}.
