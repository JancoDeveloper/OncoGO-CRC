Resumen

Immunoglobulin J chain (Joining chain of multimeric IgA and IgM) Gen en la corrida: JCHAIN. Gen en UniProt: JCHAIN. Revisada: reviewed. Rai 2025Data-Independent Acquisition Analysis to Identify Serum Biomarkers and Neoantigens for Differentiating Colorectal Cancer from Inflammatory Bowel Diseases 10.1021/acs.jproteome.5c00285

Función

FUNCTION: Serves to link two monomer units of either IgM or IgA. In the case of IgM, the J chain-joined dimer is a nucleating unit for the IgM pentamer, and in the case of IgA it induces dimers and/or larger polymers. It also helps to bind these immunoglobulins to secretory component. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P01592}.
