Resumen

Phosphatidylinositol-glycan-specific phospholipase D (PI-G PLD) (EC 3.1.4.50) (Glycoprotein phospholipase D) (Glycosyl-phosphatidylinositol-specific phospholipase D) (GPI-PLD) (GPI-specific phospholipase D) Gen en la corrida: GPLD1. Gen en UniProt: GPLD1. Revisada: reviewed. Rai 2025Data-Independent Acquisition Analysis to Identify Serum Biomarkers and Neoantigens for Differentiating Colorectal Cancer from Inflammatory Bowel Diseases 10.1021/acs.jproteome.5c00285

Función

FUNCTION: Hydrolyzes the inositol phosphate linkage of glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)-anchored membrane proteins, thereby releasing them from the cell surface. Cleaves on the inositol side of the anchor, leaving the phosphate group attached to the membrane (PubMed:17720976, PubMed:2760042). This release of GPI-anchored proteins can modulate diverse biological processes, including the activation of signaling pathways (PubMed:17720976). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17720976, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2760042}.
