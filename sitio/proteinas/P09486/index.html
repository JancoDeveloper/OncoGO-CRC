Resumen

SPARC (Basement-membrane protein 40) (BM-40) (Osteonectin) (ON) (Secreted protein acidic and rich in cysteine) Gen en la corrida: SPARC. Gen en UniProt: SPARC. Revisada: reviewed. Rai 2025Data-Independent Acquisition Analysis to Identify Serum Biomarkers and Neoantigens for Differentiating Colorectal Cancer from Inflammatory Bowel Diseases 10.1021/acs.jproteome.5c00285

Función

FUNCTION: Appears to regulate cell growth through interactions with the extracellular matrix and cytokines. Binds calcium and copper, several types of collagen, albumin, thrombospondin, PDGF and cell membranes. There are two calcium binding sites; an acidic domain that binds 5 to 8 Ca(2+) with a low affinity and an EF-hand loop that binds a Ca(2+) ion with a high affinity.
